Synchronix’s breakthrough integrates fundamental laws of physics directly into the neural network architecture—a paradigm known as Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs). By embedding thermodynamics, fluid dynamics, and stress mechanics into the calibration feedback loop, Synchronix's platform allows sensors to self-correct real-time drift with unprecedented accuracy, even in extreme conditions
